# Memorial pilot: source-linked tributes

This folder holds independent trial memorials for the **existing RIP-UK2 project**. Do not present unapproved tributes as family-authored. This first trial is for Presley Walker Gerber, not a UK death notice.

- [Presley Walker Gerber](presley-walker-gerber.md) — completed sourced Markdown draft.
- Companion site file: `public/memorials/presley-walker-gerber.htm` (built by Vite when the app is deployed). Set `noindex` pending rights/privacy and editorial review.
- The trial intentionally does **not** modify the React app's existing sample notices, routing, storage or verification badges.
- Site integration, preview deployment, family correction/contact policy, moderation and any memorial submission feature all remain **pending**.
- Importing a public tribute into the notice list must not invent a UK county, postcode, funeral, charity, authorisation, verified badge or candle count.
- The current demonstration seed notices need their provenance checked: a sample's claim to be verified is **not evidence** of a genuine obituary.
- Review `src/utils/markdownGenerator.ts` before production use: it currently fills absent birth years, verification labels, funeral details and social-post details with defaults; those are unsuitable for real memorials.

## Proposed repeatable model

1. Confirm identity, dates and death with independent reporting and preferably primary sources.
2. Capture links to **original memorials and family-authorised tributes**, quoting minimally; never assume publication permits copying images or full letters.
3. Write a concise original celebration of life with dated source list, editorial ownership and corrections/removal channel.
4. Distinguish **family-authored**, **public-source independent** and **community-submitted, unverified** tributes.
5. Use original or properly licensed graphic artwork, not a synthetic photograph presented as authentic.
6. AI memory assistance remains optional, clearly labelled, private and consent-conscious. Help visitors reflect and record their own stories, never falsely present AI as the deceased.
7. Personalised Mini-AI-me for a deceased real person must not be presumed approved by their relatives. Link only after an actual reviewed, suitably labelled asset exists.
8. Avoid grief-targeted advertising or monetisation next to death notices. Add update/takedown procedures before indexing.
9. Human approval before public promotion; test mobile and desktop; check each original link.
