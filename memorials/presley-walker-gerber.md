# Presley Walker Gerber (1999–2026)
*Remembered with love.*

Presley Walker Gerber was an American model and photographer, and the eldest child of Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber. His younger sister was Kaia Gerber. He was born on 2 July 1999 and died on 20 September 2026, aged 27.

Presley modelled for major fashion houses and also spoke publicly about the importance of mental-health awareness. Those achievements are part of his story, but they are not all of it. The clearest record of the person his family knew is in the words his family chose to share.

On 18 October 2026, Cindy Crawford, Rande Gerber and Kaia Gerber published a family tribute remembering Presley's kindness, openness and the love they continue to hold for him. Kaia published a separate letter reflecting on her brother, the childhood they shared, and the unfamiliar experience of being his sister without being able to telephone him.

This independent memorial does not claim to speak for his family. It offers a quiet place to read their original tributes, remember his life, and follow reliable sources.
