Presley Walker Gerber

2 July 1999 · 20 September 2026

Remembered with love

Presley was a model, photographer, son, and older brother. He died on 20 September 2026, aged 27. This memorial gathers verified reporting and links to the tributes written by the people closest to him.

A life, not just a headline

Presley Walker Gerber was the eldest child of Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber, and the older brother of Kaia Gerber. His modelling work included international fashion brands, and he spoke publicly about mental-health awareness.

On 18 October 2026, his parents and sister shared a joint tribute about the kindness, openness and love they remembered in him. Kaia also wrote an intimate letter about their childhood and what it means to be his sister in a world where she can no longer call him.

This is a third-party remembrance, not an official family memorial. The words below link to original posts, rather than reproducing private grief or copyrighted photographs.
